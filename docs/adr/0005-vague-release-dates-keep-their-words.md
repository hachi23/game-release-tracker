# A vague release date keeps its words and gets no countdown

IGDB stores a game announced for "2027" or "Q4 2026" with a timestamp at the end of that period (December 31). Treating that timestamp as the release day made the app promise "Arrives in 461 days" and put the game on the calendar on New Year's Eve. The date's displayed text now decides its precision (Exact, Month, Window or Year), and only an Exact date gets a countdown or a calendar cell. The timestamp is still kept for sorting, so a vague date sorts after the exact dates in its period.
